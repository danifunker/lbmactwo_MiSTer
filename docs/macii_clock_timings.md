# Macintosh II Clock Timings

Reference derived from the [Snow emulator](https://github.com/danielgwilson/snow) (`core/src/mac/macii/bus.rs`)
and verified against the FPGA implementation.

## Clock Domains

| Clock Domain       | Base       | Divider | Frequency         | Notes                                          |
|--------------------|------------|---------|-------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| System Clock       | —          | —       | 16 MHz            | Mac II main oscillator (15.6672 MHz real HW)    |
| FPGA System Clock  | 50 MHz PLL | —       | 32.5 MHz          | Drives all logic; CPU/peripherals via enables   |
| CPU Clock          | 32.5 MHz   | ÷2      | 16.25 MHz         | clk16_en_p/n (status_turbo=1)                   |
| Bus Phase (clk8)   | 32.5 MHz   | ÷4      | 8.125 MHz         | clk8_en_p/n; 4-phase bus cycle                  |
| E Clock            | CPU clock  | ÷10     | ~812.5 KHz        | E_div=1 halves eCntr advance rate               |
| VIA Clock          | 16 MHz     | ÷20     | 800 KHz           | Snow: dividers on logic board                   |
| ASC Sample Rate    | 32.5 MHz   | ÷1460   | ~22,260 Hz        | CLOCK_RATE=0; ÷2921 for ~11,127 Hz              |
| Pixel Clock        | 32.5 MHz   | accum   | 30.24 MHz         | Accumulator: fires when acc+30240 >= 32500      |
| Video Frame        | 30.24 MHz  | 896×525 | ~64.3 Hz          | 640×480 visible, 896×525 total                  |
| VBlank             | 16 MHz     | ÷60     | 60 Hz             | Snow: CLOCK_SPEED / 60                          |
| IWM/Floppy         | —          | —       | 7 or 8 MHz        | Register-selectable (IWM mode bit 4)            |
| SWIM Base          | —          | —       | 16 MHz            | Snow: Swim::new(..., 16_000_000)                |

## Bus Cycle Phases (addrController_top)

The 32.5 MHz system clock is divided into 4-phase bus cycles by a 2-bit `busPhase` counter:

| busPhase | busCycle | Bus Owner | Purpose            |
|----------|----------|-----------|---------------------|
| 00       | 00       | Video     | Video RAM read      |
| 01       | 01       | CPU       | CPU read/write      |
| 10       | 10       | I/O       | IWM/floppy/audio    |
| 11       | 11       | CPU       | CPU read/write      |

Clock enables derived from busPhase:
- `clk8_en_p` = busPhase == 2'b11 (one pulse per 4 sys clocks = 8.125 MHz)
- `clk8_en_n` = busPhase == 2'b01
- `clk16_en_p` = !busPhase[0] (one pulse per 2 sys clocks = 16.25 MHz)
- `clk16_en_n` = busPhase[0]

## E Clock Generation (tg68k.v)

The E clock is generated by a 4-bit counter (`eCntr`, 0–9) that advances on `phi2 & en_E`:

- `E_div=0`: `en_E` always 1 → eCntr advances every phi2 → E = phi2_rate / 10
- `E_div=1`: `en_E` toggles every phi1 → eCntr advances every other phi2 → E = phi2_rate / 20

With status_turbo=1: phi2 = clk16_en_n = 16.25 MHz, E_div=1 → E = 16.25/20 = **812.5 KHz**

E goes high at eCntr==5, low at eCntr==9 (6 high, 4 low duty cycle).
